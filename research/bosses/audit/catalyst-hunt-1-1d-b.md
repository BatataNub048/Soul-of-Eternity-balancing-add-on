# Phase 1.1D-B — Catalyst and Calamity Hunt

CatalystMod 1.1.8 contributes **Astrageldon and the Ascended Astral Slime miniboss**. CalamityHunt 1.2.3 contributes **Goozma**. The two bosses have native Boss Checklist entries. Ascended Astral Slime has a scoped native Checklist absence and a separate Calamity health-bar declaration. The census now has **144 confirmed groups and 30 pending catalogue records**. ## Catalyst

- **Astrageldon (1290).** One NPC runs both phases, including the Nebula form. Its AI sets the phase-one flag on reaching phase two and records full completion separately. `Astrageldon.AI` creates `NovaSlime` and `NovaSlimer` during combat. `CatalystNPC.EditSpawnPool` also allows Nova Slime as a surface Astral enemy after the full clear. Underground Metanova Slime is another post-clear ambient enemy and creates its own `MetanovaSlimeRocks` helper. Wulfrum Slime and Wulfrum Mine have their own ordinary spawn logic and use the abstract `SuperChargeEnemy` base. These ambient enemies and helpers add no confirmed major encounters.
- **Summon.** `AstralCommunicator.CanUseItem` checks surface height, Calamity's Astral zone or the CalValEX Astral Blight zone, no existing Astrageldon or spawner projectile, and Boss Rush inactive. After Moon Lord, a world needs the full Astrageldon clear to use the item. The phase-one flag enables alternate use. After that unlock, ordinary use starts the phase-two shortcut and alternate use requests the full fight. The item creates `AstrageldonSpawner`, which spawns the same NPC in either case. The ordinary route remains subject to the world's saved flags and biome access.
- **Ascended Astral Slime.** Independent hostile AI and a normal spawn route support one miniboss. `CatalystNPC.EditSpawnPool` adds it at weight 0.02 in surface Astral Infection when the player's inventory lacks an Astral Communicator. The hook excludes safe players, Abyss, Sunken Sea, Pumpkin Moon and Frost Moon and requires the Calamity Astral zone. The miniboss supplies the communicator via `AscendedAstralSlime.ModifyNPCLoot`. Catalyst explicitly calls Calamity's `DeclareMiniboss` for this NPC. The pinned API adds it to `BossHealthBarManager.MinibossHPBarList`. The declaration affects health-bar handling. The native Boss Checklist path contains only Astrageldon's `LogBoss`. The catalogue captures boss-flagged NPCs and has no Ascended Astral Slime snapshot row.

## Calamity Hunt

- **Goozma (1448).** `Goozma.Phase` uses its own AI state through the fight. `PluripotentSpawn` is the timed summon precursor. Goozma cycles `EbonianBehemuck`, `CrimulanGlopstrosity`, `DivineGargooptuar` and `StellarGeliath` as subordinate slimes and creates `Goozmite` during the fight. These cover the seven ModNPC types in the retained screen. The Slime Monsoon message and effects are part of the summon and fight. The statue starts Terraria's Slime Rain, an existing census group.
- **Statue and egg.** `SlimeNinjaStatueTile.RightClick` checks that Goozma is absent and that at least four Slime Statues are in the search rectangle extending 40 tiles horizontally and 30 vertically from the statue anchor. With `PluripotentSpawnEgg` in inventory it requests the precursor, which later spawns Goozma. The egg is the native Checklist summon item. This branch has no explicit Hardmode, Plantera or prior-Goozma flag check. Practical egg acquisition and its use in the pinned pack remain unverified.
- **Slime Rain route.** The same statue can consume `GelatinousCatalyst` to start a Slime Rain when none is active and the player lacks the egg. During Slime Rain, `GoozmaSystem.SpawnGoozmaOld` checks Hardmode or the Plantera clear, then looks for the first active boss King Slime or Queen Slime. Dropped Hunt or Calamity `OverloadedSludge` intersecting that NPC triggers the conversion. The code deactivates the slime boss and spawns `PluripotentSpawn` on authoritative execution. This route can supply the same encounter without egg possession.
- **Calamity integration.** Hunt's `BossRushInjection` removes Old Duke from the Boss Rush list and inserts Goozma after Supreme Calamitas when that anchor is present. Calamity's pinned getter and setter use the tuple shape passed by Hunt. The injection's participant lists include the five combat helpers. The normal Old Duke fight and its Checklist entry are outside this change. `CodebreakerInjection` exposes a dialogue option gated by the Goozma clear. Hunt's Infernum option affects AI parameter selection through `ConditionalValue`, and Hunt adds an Infernum intro card. Neither is a native Checklist availability predicate.
