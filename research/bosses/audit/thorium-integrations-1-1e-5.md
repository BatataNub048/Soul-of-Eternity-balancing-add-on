## Ragnarok

The 22 GlobalNPC types cover seven Thorium boss families and their existing combat actors, combat defaults and loot, and an Archmage shop hook. `OtherModsCompat.shouldRagnarokBossAILoad` accepts explicit Ragnarok or Auto when ThoriumRework is absent. The pin contains no ThoriumRework. The seven AI selectors default to Auto. The main replacement `PreAI` paths check Revengeance or Boss Rush along with their family selector. `CalamityGamemodeCheck` reads Calamity’s `GetDifficultyActive` for Revengeance, Death and Boss Rush. Death and Boss Rush alter individual attack schedules. Default declarations establish eligibility. Effective difficulty and config remain runtime inputs.

**Boss Rush.** `AddThoriumBossRush.PostSetupContent` requires Calamity, explicit Ragnarok `bossrush` selection and ThoriumRework absent. Auto is the declared default and does not pass that check. It reads and rewrites Calamity’s roster through `GetBossRushEntries` and `SetBossRushEntries` (Calamity `ModCalls.Call`, IL_1EAD and IL_1EC2). It copies existing tuples and conditionally inserts Thunder Bird, Queen Jellyfish, Viscount, Buried Champion, Granite Energy Storm, Star Scouter, Borean Strider, Fallen Beholder, Lich, Forgotten One and a shared Primordials stage beside matched base roster anchors. The tuples carry spawn delegates and stage-specific NPC lists, including cleanup exceptions. Thorium’s legacy aliases resolve the older jellyfish names used in the tuples. The Primordials delegate explicitly summons Slag Fury, Aquaius and Omnicide, with Dream Eater used as the tuple’s entry type. These stages reuse existing encounter groups. The method catches setup failures without reporting them. BC-015 needs the final roster, delegates and stage membership when the optional writer is active alongside Infernum and Hunt. The observed roster and the original Checklist declarations are separate evidence. [E1 roster evidence](review-1-1e-1.md) · [Hunt roster review](review-1-1d-b.md)

**Progression and rewards.** `BossProgressionFix.AddRecipes/PostAddRecipes` applies configurable summon-item recipe changes, including the Desert Medallion, Cryo Key, Star Caller and Overloaded Sludge. This can affect acquisition without changing the summon’s NPC identity. Retain the approved Calamity/Fables and Cryogen findings. `BossStatTweak` and the immunity/vulnerability hooks modify existing combat properties, including Patch Werk. `RagnarokGlobalNPC.OnSpawn` resets player Bloodflare bloodlust on boss spawn. Its hit and loot hooks change combat modifiers and add a boss drop. `NewBossDropsEasy.ModifyNPCLoot` adds rules to existing Thorium and Calamity kills, including Desert Scourge, Crabulon and the Ares, Thanatos and Apollo branches. The Exo reward branches read Calamity defeat flags. The search found no replacement progression flag or Checklist effect. These are recipe and reward observations, with actual acquisition and stacked drops unverified.

## CalamityBardHealer

- **`NPCChanges` is a conditional Thorium AI integration.** `AppliesToEntity` returns false when either RagnarokMod or ThoriumRework is loaded. Both `OnSpawn` and `AI` are behind this hook, so its custom AI is gated out in the pinned pack by Ragnarok. In a profile without both mods, it selects Thorium bosses and named helpers, initializes Fallen Beholder AI on Revengeance spawns, and uses Calamity Revengeance and Death state to change attacks or run extra native AI passes. `UpdateCustomAIs` includes Thunder Bird and hatchling, Buried Champion, Star Scouter, Granite Energy Storm, Beholder and both Fallen Beholder forms, and Slag Fury, Aquaius, Omnicide and Dream Eater. Difficulty, Master Mode and the special seed affect timing. This code reuses the existing fights.
- **`NPCBalancing` is independent of that exclusion.** It targets damaging Thorium NPCs in its NPC namespace outside the boss namespace, excluding AI style 7, sets combat defaults and optionally calls Calamity `SetDefenseDamageNPC`. Its `ModifyNPCLoot` adds a post-Golem Scoria rule to named Thorium sea enemies. The Golem flag is a read for loot eligibility.
- **`NPCDrops` is also separate.** It adjusts Bard/Healer drops for existing Calamity NPCs, including Desert Scourge, Crabulon and Cryogen, and adds progression-conditioned loot such as the Aureus-gated Star Cluster rule. `SetStaticDefaults` calls `WhisperingSetQuotesGlobalNPC.RegisterQuote` by reflection for named bosses. These are reward and presentation effects. The inspected hooks supplied no spawn or defeat flag write, new phase identity, Checklist call or pool amendment.

Ragnarok and CalamityBardHealer therefore have overlapping AI targets with an explicit gate against running both custom AI implementations. Their separate combat and loot hooks can still affect the same fight. Ragnarok’s `OtherModsCompat.PostAddRecipes` also negotiates equipment recipe duplication with CalamityBardHealer. No comparable encounter hook emerged from Thoriamity. No evidence changes the accepted identity mapping for the four Calamity/Fables fights, Infernum/Exo Mechs or Fargo/Cryogen. Effective loot and combat effects remain unmeasured.

## Thorium Patch Werk

**Keep `ThoriumMod/PatchWerk` as one confirmed miniboss.** `PatchWerk.SpawnChance` returns weight 0.2 during Blood Moon above the surface, outside the sky spawn category, with its defeat flag clear and no active Patch Werk. `OnKill` sets `ThoriumWorld.downedPatchWerk`, saved as `patchwerk`, and requests world-data broadcast on the server on first defeat. `FreshBrain.CanUseItem` requires Blood Moon and absence of Patch Werk, with no first-clear restriction on item use. Its local-player use calls `NPCHelper.NPCSpawnOnPlayer` for the same NPC. Thorium’s `ConfusedZombie.AddShops` (IL_02C1–02D4) stocks Fresh Brain under `ThoriumConditions.DownedPatchWerk`. The native shop route is for repeat summons.

- **Standalone declaration.** Thorium’s `DoBossChecklistSupport_LogBosses` uses `LogMiniBoss` with key `ThoriumMod PatchWerk`, progression value 2.51, the single Patch Werk type and predicate `ThoriumWorld.downedPatchWerk`. The version gate is 1.6 or later. Metadata names Fresh Brain and Bloody Tear. Bloody Tear starts the event and Fresh Brain directly requests the miniboss within it.
- **Static Blood Moon pool.** `BossTracker.EventNPCs["Terraria BloodMoon"]` contains 18 vanilla types including Dreadnautilus (618). Blood Moon has no entry in the invasion-set mapping used by `FinalizeEventNPCPools`. Thorium’s `DoBossChecklistSupport_SubmitEventNPCs` includes Abomination, BloodDrop, BloodMage, BloodyWarg, FamishedMaggot, BurstingMaggot, EngorgedEye, GraveLimb, SeveredLegs and SmotheringShade. Patch Werk is absent from both lists. `BossLogSystem.AddRecipes` finalizes invasion pools and then applies queued orphan data. `FinalizeOrphanData` adds submitted NPC IDs to the corresponding event entry. Registering the standalone miniboss supplies no implicit amendment to the Blood Moon pool.
- **Finalized membership.** No amendment surfaced in the three integrations or retained named-call evidence. Boss Checklist supports submissions from other mods, so the effective full-pack pool remains **UNVERIFIED**. Capture `npcIDs` for `Terraria BloodMoon` and `ThoriumMod PatchWerk` after `BossLogSystem.AddRecipes`, compare with the loaded Patch Werk type and record registration category and book visibility separately. An additional standalone declaration would duplicate the existing encounter. An explicit Blood Moon membership amendment would address an event association. BC-004 retains only the unresolved finalized question for Patch Werk.
