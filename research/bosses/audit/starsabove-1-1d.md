# Phase 1.1D-A — The Stars Above encounter audit

The Stars Above 2.1.8.4 adds **nine distinct bosses and one world event** to the static census. The mod declares nine native Boss Checklist entries, all as bosses. Two entries have availability callbacks. Everlasting Light is the one verified event and has a scoped native registration absence. No standalone miniboss was confirmed. The three NPC leads in the companion matrix stay outside the confirmed count. Finalized registration, book visibility and actual access in the maintained pack remain **UNVERIFIED**. The audit reused the Phase 1.1A `StarsAbove.tsv`, pinned `StarsAbove.dll`, and decompiled Boss Checklist dispatcher and tracker. Prior screening found 48 ModNPC types including abstract and inherited types, and 14 direct GlobalNPC types. The focused source pass checked NPC families, spawn paths, spawn pool hooks, event systems and native Checklist calls. ## Native Boss Checklist calls

`StarsAbove.StarsAbove.PostSetupContent` calls Boss Checklist 2.2.4 only when the mod lookup succeeds. The dispatcher receives the Stars Above mod instance, internal suffix, completion callback, NPC argument and extra metadata. The canonical form is `StarsAbove <suffix>`. The ordinary pack has the dependency pinned. The two explicit availability callbacks remain separate from the completion callbacks and acquisition gates. The other seven calls supply no entry-specific availability predicate. Neither this setup method nor the other native Checklist reference in `WarriorOfLightBossFinalPhase.BossLoot` calls `LogMiniBoss` or `LogEvent`. The latter sends a message. External registration and replacement behavior outside the reviewed paths remain **UNVERIFIED**.

## Access gates and mismatches

- **Main-world summons.** Six encounters have an ordinary main-world gift or dialogue route before considering the Warrior. The CSV identifies the six items, the progression checks and the local use gates. Penthesilea's English Checklist hint names the Twins, while the unlock code accepts completed dialogue for any of the three mechanical bosses alongside the Vagrant dialogue. The Starfarer Essence gift needs Plantera and four Stars Above clears. Checklist availability uses only Plantera and Penthesilea. `TsukiyomiBoss.OnKill` also writes `downedStarfarers`, so a completion marker alone cannot establish that the Starfarer fight happened.
- **Arbitration.** A tier-three voyage to Lyra or Dreaming City can expire into Katabasis. `SubworldPlayer` grants Demonic Crux there while the anomaly timer is positive if the Arbiter and a held Crux are absent. A Crux used in Katabasis requests the boss. Outside Katabasis, ordinary use enters the arena. The server config's default-off `MultiplayerFallbackSubworlds` flag lets the Lyra or Dreaming City travel UI award the Crux and lets an MP client request the boss directly. The native Checklist declaration carries an empty metadata dictionary. Its localized hint explains the anomaly and Lyra fallback. Exact behavior on the pinned Subworld Library and Titan world remains untested.
- **Everlasting Light and the Warrior.** `EverlastingLightEvent.PostUpdateTime` counts 54,000 updates per increment after Moon Lord. Class defaults start preview at two increments and active Light at seven. `LoadWorldData` assigns thresholds from the saved tags, so the actual timeline on existing saves is unverified. Preview and active Light are stages of one event. Active Light applies player and hostile-NPC changes and suppresses the Solar Eclipse flag. The Warrior clear ends both states. Dialogue 304 can award Progenitor Wish during preview or active Light. An initial spawn needs active Light, while a cleared world allows a rematch. The event gates access to the Warrior but has no declared Checklist event or NPC membership. It does not add a second Warrior fight.
- **Tsukiyomi.** Four clear dialogues provide the Mnemonic Traces for crafting at Celestriad Root. The single-player use path enters Eternal Confluence and requests the NPC when used inside. The multiplayer client takes a direct spawn-request branch. The Checklist availability callback tests the Warrior flag alone and does not check acquisition of the remaining traces or the station. Test both the route and the copied defeat flag on subworld exit before making runtime claims.

The Stars Above code reads Calamity and Fargo completion for extra dialogue and recognizes Fables Sir Nautilus in dialogue logic. The audited Stars Above boss calls use native NPC types and native flags. The Calamity branch in `NeonVeilSurfacePass` concerns placement and is relevant to ambient enemies. Evidence for an external mod altering the nine native encounters or adding any Checklist rows for them remains **UNVERIFIED**.

## Other NPCs and encounter boundaries

The spawn-pool review included the two direct GlobalNPCs that provide biome pools. The other 12 direct GlobalNPC types are debuff or combat-effect hooks. Paracausal Enigma is a low-weight Dreaming City pool entry with its own combat AI. Asteroid Worm is an ambient pool entry in Neon Veil and several offworld biomes with one head and two segment types. Their static spawn paths justify retaining them as leads. Independent miniboss status remains **UNVERIFIED** for both. Neither has a native Checklist call. `SpectreOfLight` has an NPC definition and lore but no spawn route or event-pool assignment was located in the pin. Other pool entries serve as ordinary biome enemies, town NPCs or loot entities. `LogicVirusEnemy` creates `LogicBombEnemy` as its attack payload. `DummyEnemy` has a test spawner. These do not add to the confirmed encounter count. The timed Lyra and Dreaming City anomaly transitions are access paths to Arbitration. The reviewed hooks did not establish another independent scripted fight or invasion. [Scoped candidates](starsabove-1-1d.csv)
