## Entropy Legacy

- **Abyssal Wraith.** `VoidCultist.AI` (IL_0000–006E) decrements the actor's `noSummon` timer. With the timer expired and the actor outside Attack and Summoning states, it counts all active `VoidCultist` instances in the NPC array, including the Assassin subclass. At four it enters Summoning and requests a net update. The count does not apply the initiator's cooldown or AI-state filter to each other actor. `SummoningAI` can create the ritual projectile off-client if no circle is active, the active count is still at least four, and the initiator's timer permits it. The prior audit establishes the projectile's downstream Wraith spawn. The Wraith has an independent `OnKill` outcome via `EDownedBosses.downedAbyssalWraith`, with save/load and network fields. Neither cultist type has a natural-spawn override. The owner DLL's spawn-pool hooks and exact type/name references supplied no ordinary population caller. The ritual branch itself supplies no biome, time, progression or active-invasion check. Ordinary cultist creation, concurrent availability and ritual completion remain unverified.
- **Spirit Fountain.** The parent's combat AI creates linked `SpiritRing` NPCs. Preserve both catalogue identities on the existing parent row. `EModSys.PostDrawTiles` (IL_02E6–02F5) caches the NPC type for rendering. No initial spawn, encounter item or event caller surfaced in the pinned type/name and spawn-hook search. `CheckDead` returns true and `OnKill` returns immediately. The combat actor can die and the inspected kill handler establishes no separate saved progression outcome. First access remains unverified.
- **Void Pope.** The apparent reference in `CalamityEntropyLegacy.PostSetupContent` (IL_1C8D–1CA8) sets a boss-bar color. The Pope creates two `VoidPopeHand` combat helpers. There is no Pope `OnKill` override or traced Pope-specific clear flag or event-completion call. The same initial-spawn search found no supported source for the Pope. Its namespace alone supplies no evidence that Void Invasion spawns or gates it.
- **Delirium.** `Delirium.IsLoadingEnabled` returns false unconditionally (IL_0000–0001). The tagged-NPC detour and borrowed-form roster remain in the DLL. They establish no ordinary activation or independent reachable defeat for the disabled actor. Record a disabled lead outside the census.
- **Void Invasion.** `VoidInvasion.PostUpdateEverything` reads `Active`, sets the local player's `VortexSky` to 5 while active, and resets `Progress` to zero while inactive. The owner IL search found zero writes to `Active` and only that reset write to `Progress`. No in-assembly start, progressing roster, victory or link to any of the three bosses was established. Keep this outside the census. A pinned activation caller and an outcome would justify reconsideration.

The earlier scoped native Checklist absence for the three parents and the two leads still applies. No canonical key, external entry, finalized membership, visibility, availability predicate or actual Checklist clear is established. The Wraith's saved kill bit describes world progression. ## Kiyume Funeral Cortege

**Uncounted scripted encounter lead.** The director and yokai loading gates return true. `KiyumeHauntDirector.PostUpdateNPCs` runs its pumps off-client every 20 ticks while Kiyume is active. `PumpCortege` waits out the 14,400-tick session cooldown, checks that the fog-tide expression reaches 0.75, requires a living player and no active leader, and probes ground near tile X 2300. A selected site must be at least 400 pixels from living players. It creates one leader and requests four linked porters.

The procession has its own outcome. Reaching the destination leads to a bow, reward and disappearance. Contact, damage or sustained obstruction can trigger a turn, coffin drop and rage sequence before departure. Stalling can also end the procession. The optional coffin is part of the same sequence. `FuneralCortegeYokai.UpdateMarch/UpdateBow/UpdateSink/JudgeScare` establish the interaction and end without a world victory or saved defeat predicate in those paths. The Kiyume-specific wrapper calls `SubworldSystem.Enter<KiyumeWorld>()` at IL_0019. The exact wrapper/type and named-entry search yielded no supported player-facing caller. The script is reachable conditionally inside the subworld. Ordinary entry into Kiyume and actual completion remain **UNVERIFIED**. A normal Kiyume entrance would justify evaluating this as one special encounter, with Checklist role still open.
