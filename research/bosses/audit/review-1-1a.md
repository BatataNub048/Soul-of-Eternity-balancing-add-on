## Major findings

1. **Two Calamity availability predicates were missed in the method marked fully reviewed.** In `WeakReferenceSupport.AddCalamityBosses`, Hive Mind supplies `!WorldGen.crimson || Main.drunkWorld` and the Perforators supply `WorldGen.crimson || Main.drunkWorld` as the `availability` delegate. Both rows say `CONFIRMED_REGISTERED` with prerequisites unverified. Vanilla's equivalent world-evil predicates were counted as conditional. For the **same 131 rows and original register scopes**, correcting these two alone gives **94 / 8 / 28 / 1**. All eight have source declarations. Boss Checklist stores a callback and later evaluates it with filters such as `HideUnavailable` and downed state. Future totals should distinguish a condition on making the call from a condition on availability after the call. This correction is conclusive from the assemblies. [Hive Mind and Perforators, lines 71–72](encounters.csv#L71) · [Vanilla comparison, lines 15–16](encounters.csv#L15) · [Existing conditional rows, lines 76 and 92–93](encounters.csv#L76)

2. **Infernum declares standalone entries for Dreadnautilus and Primordial Wyrm.** The original narrow absences hold in the vanilla initializer and Calamity's own register. In pinned Infernum 2.0.1.35, the very method named in the matrix, `BossChecklistManagementSystem.PostSetupContent`, constructs `LogBoss` from `"Log" + type`. With Boss Checklist loaded, it submits NPC 618 under `InfernumMode Dreadnautilus` and Calamity's `PrimordialWyrmHead` under `InfernumMode PrimordialWyrm`. Calamity also supplies a separate spawn path in `WorldMiscUpdateSystem.TrySpawnAEoW`, which tests Chaos State and the Abyss or zenith world among its guards. Both Infernum availability callbacks require `WorldSaveSystem.InfernumModeEnabled && Main.hardMode`. The same method declares `InfernumMode BereftVassal` with both the Vassal and Great Sand Shark and a different availability predicate. Retain row 150's pending encounter disposition while recording its positive registration evidence. These are external source declarations for existing records. Adding another encounter for each registrar would double-count them. [Matrix, line 70](mod-coverage.csv#L70) · [Dreadnautilus, line 41](encounters.csv#L41) · [Wyrm, line 103](encounters.csv#L103) · [Vassal, line 150](encounters.csv#L150) · [Gap summary, line 15](checkpoint.md#L15)

3. **Echdeath's horizontal-input condition is reversed.** In `EchdeathPandoraBoxGlobal.UseItem`, IL offsets `006B–008C` require Up and Down pressed, Left and Right released. Holding all four inputs clears the spawn flag. The item, eclipse, moon phase zero, active NPC 68, floor tile 409, wall 87, nearby painting and non-client spawn checks are present. The hook also has a dependency on the pinned `FargowiltasSoulsDLC`. This confirms a coded spawn path. Echdeath's standalone book registration remains unresolved. ## Minor findings

- **Several vanilla registration keys are initializer arguments.** `EntryInfo.MakeVanillaBoss` and `MakeVanillaEvent` normalize the name, and the constructor prefixes the owner. For example, `NPCName.EaterofWorldsHead` becomes `Terraria EaterofWorlds` and `Bestiary_Events.BloodMoon` becomes `Terraria BloodMoon`. The four pillar rows put the parent event label in the standalone-key column. Keep initializer arguments separately if useful. Use the canonical key and relationship when comparing with the runtime entry dictionary. [Blood Moon, line 3](encounters.csv#L3) · [Eater of Worlds, line 15](encounters.csv#L15) · [Pillars, lines 61–64](encounters.csv#L61)
- **Event membership can already be resolved for three priority NPCs.** `BossTracker.EventNPCs` supplies 618 for Blood Moon and 477 for Eclipse to `MakeVanillaEvent`. For Goblin Army, `FinalizeEventNPCPools` uses `NPCID.Sets.BelongsToInvasionGoblinArmy`, which includes 471 in this tModLoader build. All three lack built-in standalone entries. Membership establishes neither an individual completion flag nor visibility. Patch Werk also has a natural Blood Moon spawn predicate, despite its empty parent field. Keep that spawn association separate from Thorium’s standalone miniboss declaration. [Vanilla, lines 41 and 45–46](encounters.csv#L41) · [Patch Werk, line 108](encounters.csv#L108) · [Coverage, line 42](coverage.md#L42)
- **THE LORDE has reachable code paths beyond its AI.** `NO.CanUseItem/UseItem` checks for an existing THE LORDE and Boss Rush, then summons it. Independently, `Bohldohr.SpawnChance` uses the Jungle Temple spawn condition and `Bohldohr.OnKill` calls `SpawnOnPlayer` on a 1-in-42 check in a zenith world outside multiplayer-client mode. The audit already retains it as a candidate. Record the special-seed route. Ordinary-seed item acquisition and successful access in this pack remain unverified. [THE LORDE, line 104](encounters.csv#L104)

A search for literal messages misses dynamically assembled calls such as Infernum's and Fargo's. A wrapper such as CalamityOverhaul's `BossLogRegistry.Register` receives the target from its caller. Direct type counts and boss flags miss spawns in global item/NPC hooks, non-boss ModNPCs, and events in mod systems with no boss NPC. A boss flag can also describe a dummy or segment. Distinguish origin, registrar, source call, availability, event membership, access and observed visibility. For preliminary matrix rows, require a named method or preserve uncertainty before asserting encounter modification. For example, line 72 cites metadata screening for TitanWorlds while the project documents a world-generation change.
