## CalValEX

### Shared Jharim route

The craftable `JharimHead` uses one Gold or Platinum Ore at a Work Bench. Both `CalValEXRecipes.AddRecipes` predicates allow crafting in April Fools week or a Zenith world. The item's own `CanUseItem` separately requires `AprilFoolMonth` and no existing Jharim. `UseItem` requests the town NPC on the player. Crafting eligibility alone does not establish item use. `CalValEX.Load` sets date flags from the local calendar (April 1–7 for the week, April for the month) or optional CalValPlus, which is absent from the lock. With `AprilFoolsContent` on, `CalValEXWorld.PreUpdateNPCs` forces the flags in drunk worlds. With it off, the update clears them. The binary defaults it to on. [Pack lock](../../../modpack/profiles/TodosRecuperados-Titanico-Desempenho.lock.json)

Other Jharim paths exist. `CanTownNPCSpawn` with Calamity requires `TownNPC` false, April Fools week and saved `jharim` history. Opening Calamity's Starter Bag checks the week flag, no Jharim, the local opener and vanity drops enabled. Jharim, Fogbound and Meldosaurus deactivate outside the month flag in the pinned Calamity context unless the local player's `Blok` is active. The Oracle sells Cube, whose pet buff sets that flag. This provides an exception to AI deactivation. `JharimHead.CanUseItem` still checks the month flag. There is no traced biome requirement for the three initial Jharim-based spawn calls. Existing-world access and any solo/multiplayer differences remain open.

### Fogbound — snapshot 1452

`Jharim.OnChatButtonClicked` uses the second button while Jharim is outside the Meldosaurus transformation. With Calamity active, the local interaction checks the `scal` and `draedon` downed calls (Supreme Calamitas and Exo Mechs), deactivates Jharim and creates Fogbound (NPC type reference IL_00E7). Fogbound then runs its own fight. Its fists and laser are projectiles. In daytime the AI deactivates it at or below half life, so an ordinary clear should account for the night window. `OnKill` sets `CalValEXWorld.downedFogbound`, included in save/load and network serialization. `CalValEXGlobalNPC.ModifyNPCLoot` defines Purified Fog and conditional first-clear Knowledge when `DisableVanityDrops` is false. The independent kill handler still sets the downed flag. This supports one standalone boss. The daytime deactivation is separate from a kill. [Focused row](calvalex-whips-1-1e-2.csv)

### Meldosaurus — snapshot 1454

In the pinned Calamity branch, `Jharim.OnHitByProjectile` ignores Shimmer Jharim and sets `MELDOSAURUSED` on a `CosmicFire` hit. CalValEX resolves that projectile as `CalProjectileID.MeldFlames` and returns true for that projectile against Jharim in `CalValEXGlobalProjectile.CanHitNPC`. The pinned tModLoader damage path consumes the nullable hit override. Calamity's `DeadSunsWind` fires `CosmicFire` using gel. Its registered recipe takes 18 Meld Blobs at the Ancient Manipulator. Astrum Deus loot includes Meld Blobs. This traces a legitimate weapon and ammo route to the transformation. `Jharim.AI` then reaches tick 360, creates Meldosaurus (NPC type reference IL_0372) and deactivates Jharim. The nearby Supreme Calamitas interaction is a separate branch with separate projectiles. The transformation does not test that NPC or require the two defeat flags used by Fogbound.

Meldosaurus uses its own combat AI. It creates two `MeldWyrmHead` attack actors in the same sequence, with the Meldosaurus index passed at creation. `MeldWyrmHead.SpawnSegments` creates the body and tail with head linkage. The clone and Amogus actors in this path are projectiles. Count one boss. `OnKill` sets the saved `downedMeldosaurus` flag and calls `UpdateWorldBool`, which requests a world-data broadcast on the server. Synchronization remains unverified. When `DisableVanityDrops` is false, the global loot hook defines normal weapons or an Expert bag, plus vanity and first-clear knowledge. The kill flag is independent of this loot gate. Ordinary projectile collision under the full pack, the cutscene spawn and persisted defeat credit remain unobserved.

### Orthocera Apparition — outside the snapshot

This separate noncombat encounter has a gated natural spawn and its own one-time outcome. `OrthoceraApparition.SpawnChance` returns weight 5 in the Sulphurous Sea with Calamity loaded, Supreme Calamitas and Exo Mechs cleared, no active apparition and `orthofound` false. The actor has zero contact damage, is invulnerable and rejects item and projectile hits. A player-relative position check starts its scripted movement and disappearance. At timer 210 its AI registers Bestiary credit, deactivates the actor, sets `CalValEXWorld.orthofound` (IL_0209) and requests synchronization. The saved flag suppresses subsequent spawns. The one-time scripted sighting earns one special-encounter group and has no combat clear. Actual encounter and persisted sighting credit remain unobserved.

## Unofficial Calamity Whips

**Scraper of Stars — snapshot 1548.** `RightousDawn.AddRecipes` registers the summon item when Calamity is loaded, using 18 Stardust Fragments at the Lunar Crafting Station (item 3459 and tile 412 in the matching tModLoader). In `Shoot`, an existing head blocks another request. Otherwise it calls `NPC.NewNPCDirect` 250 pixels to the right of the player's center (IL_0086) and returns false to suppress the ordinary projectile. This player item path has no direct biome, seed, downed flag or net-mode test in the method. The ingredients and station give it a Lunar tier route. The final recipe state and multiplayer behavior require observation.

The head requests a 50-actor chain including itself, 48 body segments and one tail. `WormHead.HeadAI_SpawnSegments` builds it off-client and deactivates the chain if the expected count fails. `SpawnSegment` sets `realLife` to the head and links adjacent segments. The tail creates `ScraperOfStarsDisjoint` off-client when its target is within 500 pixels and the cooldown permits. Disjoints home independently, release hostile projectiles on player contact and kill themselves. They are attack helpers with no separate entry conditions or clear. The head has defeat text and a guaranteed `TrueStarScraper` loot rule. No mod-specific saved defeat flag was traced. Count one independent boss.
