## Orthocera Apparition and intended Checklist scope

A cosmetic town pet or an ambient NPC with only a spawn weight would lack this evidence. **No count correction is required. Recommend Bestiary-only tracking for Orthocera.** Its scripted observation already has a completion record, and no source establishes an intended Checklist objective.
