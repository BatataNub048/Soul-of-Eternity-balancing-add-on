## Summon conditions and wording corrections

- **Astrageldon.** The communicator requires surface height and either Calamity's Astral zone or CalValEX's Astral Blight. Boss Rush, an active Astrageldon or an active Astrageldon spawner blocks use. The matrix and inventory phrase “no active boss” should read “no active Astrageldon.” After Moon Lord, the world must already have the full Astrageldon clear. Reaching phase two sets the separate phase-one flag. Once unlocked, ordinary use requests the phase-two shortcut and alternate use requests the full fight. Both paths spawn the same NPC. See `AstralCommunicator.cs`, lines 38–85, `AstrageldonSpawner.cs`, lines 258–274, and `Astrageldon.cs`, lines 1131–1139, 2187–2190, 2925 and 4714.
- **Ascended Astral Slime.** `CatalystNPC.EditSpawnPool`, lines 370–395, assigns weight 0.02 in surface Astral Infection when the player's inventory lacks a communicator. Safe players, Abyss, Sunken Sea, Pumpkin Moon and Frost Moon are excluded. The value is a pool weight. The communicator has its own guaranteed drop rule. The Astrum Aureus condition in the same loot chain applies to the staff.
- **Goozma, statue route.** Right-clicking the Slime Ninja Statue checks for an absent Goozma and at least four Slime Statues within ±40 tiles horizontally and ±30 vertically. Possessing the egg requests the precursor and retains the egg. This branch has no explicit difficulty, Hardmode, Plantera or prior-clear check. Acquisition is a separate question. The egg recipe requires five Chromatic Mass, and Goozma and its treasure bag supply that material. The evidence therefore supports repeat use but does not establish fresh-world egg acquisition before the first kill. Without an egg, the valid arrangement can consume one Gelatinous Catalyst to start an inactive Slime Rain. See `SlimeNinjaStatueTile.RightClick` and `SummonPluripotentSpawn`, `GoozmaSystem.FindSlimeStatues`, `PluripotentSpawnEgg.AddRecipes`, `Goozma.cs` line 418 and `TreasureTrunk.cs` line 44.
- **Goozma, Slime Rain route.** During Slime Rain, `SpawnGoozmaOld` requires Hardmode **or** the Plantera clear. It selects the first active boss-flagged King Slime (50) or Queen Slime (657) and checks for a dropped sludge item intersecting that NPC. In this pack it consumes one unit per intersecting Calamity sludge stack, deactivates the selected slime boss and creates the precursor on the server or in single-player. The report's “Hunt or Calamity” describes two code branches. Only `CalamityMod/OverloadedSludge` loads in this configuration. See `GoozmaSystem.cs`, lines 140–189 and 220–224, `OverloadedSludge.IsLoadingEnabled`, and pinned `CalamityMod.tsv`, line 5097.

## Compatibility and limits of acceptance

**Boss Rush.** Hunt removes Old Duke from the Boss Rush roster and inserts Goozma immediately after the Supreme Calamitas anchor in the expected list. The spawn callback requests Goozma directly. Calamity 2.2.4's getter and setter accept the same eight-field tuple. The five combat helpers occupy the protected-hostiles array. The separate `BossIDsAfterDeath` array contains Goozma alone. Hunt assumes the expected roster when indexing the list. The final order after other mods edit it is **UNVERIFIED**. This method edits only the Boss Rush roster and its associated mapping. It supplies no evidence of an ordinary Old Duke removal or Checklist edit. See `CalamityHunt.BossRushInjection` and `CalamityMod.ModCalls.decompiled.cs`, lines 2482–2520.

**Concurrent fights.** While Goozma is active, `LobotomizeAstrageldon.PreAI` damps Astrageldon's velocity and returns false for that NPC. Outside phases −1 and −23, Goozma can enter −23 when Astrageldon is present. It freezes Astrageldon and clears the public `NebulaForm` field early, then pulls it closer. Within 30 pixels it creates a zero-damage `NebulaHitbox` and sets Astrageldon's `active` flag to false. That branch contains no normal kill or full-clear call. Actual credit, loot, Checklist tracking and multiplayer behavior during absorption remain **UNVERIFIED**. See `LobotomizeAstrageldon.cs`, lines 12–37, `Goozma.cs`, lines 1199–1236 and 2468–2478, and `Astrageldon.cs`, lines 139 and 4714.

Carry the corrected sludge owner and Astrageldon-specific exclusion into future summon instructions. The counts, categories and native keys stand. Finalized dictionary membership, in-game book visibility, actual item acquisition and successful playthroughs remain **UNVERIFIED** for this batch.
