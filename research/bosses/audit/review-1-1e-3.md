## Sir Nautilus

A suitable underground-desert footprint and graveyard sites lead to a cracked sandstone trail and chamber. In `TESirNautilusSpawner.Update` an active combat actor anywhere blocks activation, and the spawner can adopt an existing passive actor anywhere. The 2,400-pixel square applies to the player hitbox check for creating a new passive actor. A particular existing world's chamber, pedestal and first access remain unverified. [Nautilus access evidence](fargo-fables-1-1e-3.md#sir-nautilus)

The wake-up introduction leads through `CompleteIntroduction`, challenge, and the first confirmation click. `SummonNautilus` requires the passive dialogue anchor. Solo creates the combat NPC there. A client sends the anchor slot to `SirNautilusSummonPacket.Receive`, which checks for an existing combat NPC before creating one and requesting its update. This establishes a legitimate first duel with no prior boss-clear prerequisite. Signathion and the rider share one NPC whose maximum life is their sum. The unmounted threshold is the rider's share of that sum.

The native `CalamityFables SirNautilus` boss entry lists only the combat NPC with default availability. It reads `SirNautilusDialogue.DefeatedNautilus`. On the usual first lethal check, `CheckDead` sets that flag **before** holding the boss at one life for the defeat cutscene. The flag has save and network serialization, with world-data loading guarded by `!NautilusDemo`. B observed the named entry.
