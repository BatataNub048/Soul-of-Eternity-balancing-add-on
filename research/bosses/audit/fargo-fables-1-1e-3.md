## Sir Nautilus

`FablesWorld.ModifyWorldGenTasks` adds the Nautilus Chamber generation pass at the Traps anchor. `SealedChamber.TryGenerate` looks for a suitable footprint beneath the desert, with graveyard spots and structure permission. It places a cracked sandstone trail and chamber. `PlaceChamber` sets the pedestal and its tile entity. Generation can fail to place a suitable chamber. Existence in a particular maintained world remains open.

`TESirNautilusSpawner.Update` creates the passive NPC if a player hitbox intersects the 2,400-pixel square around the pedestal when no active combat or reusable passive actor is there. `SirNautilusPassive.AI` opens dialogue on a fresh right-click over the actor within 400 pixels, with the local player alive and the fullscreen map closed. Wake him and advance the first introduction to reach the challenge button. `InitiateFight` opens a first-challenge confirmation textbox. Its click calls `SummonNautilus` with the passive NPC anchored. The method creates the combat actor in solo or sends `SirNautilusSummonPacket` as a client. Later challenges can advance directly. The source path makes no prior boss clear a condition of the first duel. The summon starts a combat introduction and the passive actor gives way to the active actor.

One boss NPC owns the rider and Signathion life totals. `BaseLifeMax` adds the two values and the boss uses the mounted/unmounted life threshold with collision behavior for Signathion. This is one life pool on one combat NPC. The passive actor belongs to the dialogue and post-defeat sequence. `CheckDead` records `SirNautilusDialogue.DefeatedNautilus`, which is saved and serialized, and permits a defeat cutscene before final death and passive restoration on the corresponding path. The native `CalamityFables SirNautilus` LogBoss uses just the combat type, default availability and this defeat predicate. The finalized key and named book row were observed in 1.1B. The first dialogue-to-combat transition and effective clear, reload and multiplayer state remain **UNVERIFIED**. [1.1B review](review-1-1b.md#what-the-book-showed)
